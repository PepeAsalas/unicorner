# Landing page art: prompts for ChatGPT

Four images that make the landing page one family: a calm default background, and new thumbnails for Infinity, Duels
and Chairman. Generate them **in the same chat**, one after the other, so ChatGPT keeps the style. Send me the PNGs and
I'll make the .webp files and wire them in.

## Shared style (paste this first, once)

> For the next images, use one consistent style: 16-bit pixel art, crisp square pixels, no blur, no anti-aliasing, no
> gradients except simple 2–3 step dithering, no text or letters anywhere, no logos. Thick dark outlines (#1d1a2b) on
> characters and objects. Limited palette only: ink #1d1a2b, marine #1d4486, light marine #2d5aa0, pitch green #2f7a4f,
> dark pitch green #2a6b43, gold #f2c230, cream #fbf6e8, clay red #c8473b. No pink, no purple, no neon, no rainbows.
> The unicorn character is a small white pixel unicorn with a dark navy mane and tail, a gold horn and gold hooves, always
> with a white-and-black football at its feet.

## 1. Default background (two versions)

Tall, for phones:

> A calm football pitch seen from straight above, filling the whole image: wide mowing stripes alternating between
> #2f7a4f and #2a6b43, faint cream pitch lines (halfway line and centre circle in the upper third, part of the penalty box
> at the bottom edge). Very subtle grass texture made of a few scattered darker pixels. Slightly darker towards the
> edges. Nothing else: no players, no ball, no crowd, no stands. It is a background for text and cards, so keep it quiet
> and even. Portrait, 1080 × 1920.

Wide, for computers:

> The same pitch, same colours and stripes, landscape 1920 × 1080, with the centre circle in the upper middle and the
> touchlines just visible at the left and right edges.

## Thumbnails (v2): Infinity, Duels, Chairman

The first try was too busy. These are simpler: one big character, one idea, on the same striped pitch as the new
background, so the tiles look like they belong to the page. They are shown small (about 330 × 150 on computers,
110 × 90 on phones), so the subject has to read at a glance.

Paste this first, in a new chat, together with the background image you already made (so it matches the greens):

> I'm making three small game-tile pictures for a pixel-art football site. Use the attached pitch image as the style
> and colour reference. Rules for all three: 16-bit pixel art, big crisp pixels (the picture should look like it was
> drawn at about 120 × 80 pixels and scaled up), thick dark outline #1d1a2b around every character and object, flat
> colours with at most one shade per colour, no blur, no gradients, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos. Background:
> the same vertical mowing stripes in #2f7a4f and #2a6b43 as the reference, nothing else in the background. Only these
> extra colours: white #ffffff, cream #fbf6e8, ink #1d1a2b, gold #f2c230, marine #1d4486, clay red #c8473b. The unicorn is
> chunky and cute, side view: white body, dark navy mane and tail, gold horn, gold hooves. One subject, centred, filling
> about 60% of the height, with plenty of plain pitch around it. Landscape 1200 × 800.

Then, one at a time:

**Infinity**
> The unicorn dribbling to the right, and behind it the path of the ball drawn as a thick gold dotted line that loops
> into one big infinity sign (∞). Just the unicorn, the ball and the gold loop.

**Duels**
> Two unicorns facing each other nose to nose, one wearing a gold shirt and one wearing a clay-red shirt, with one
> football between their front hooves. A tiny white spark between their horns. Nothing else.

**Chairman**
> The unicorn standing on its back legs in a marine-blue suit with a gold tie, holding a big gold trophy over its head
> with both front hooves, a football at its feet. Nothing else.

If one comes out too detailed, reply: "Simpler: fewer, bigger pixels, fewer colours, keep only the main subject."

## Banners (v2): TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit

The banners sit under the game tiles and on the results screen. The site writes the words on top ("Follow on TikTok",
"Join the board", ...) on the **left**, so the left 40% of each picture must be plain pitch. Two sizes each: wide
1200 × 300 (computers and phones) and tall 160 × 600 (side rail). Make them in one chat, after the style block below.

> I'm making three ad banners for a pixel-art football site. Use the attached pitch image as the style and colour
> reference. Rules for all three: 16-bit pixel art, big crisp pixels, thick dark outline #1d1a2b around characters and
> objects, flat colours with at most one shade per colour, no blur, no gradients, no text, no letters, no logos. The
> background is the same top-down pitch with vertical mowing stripes in #2f7a4f and #2a6b43, with nothing else on it.

> chunky and cute, side view: white body, dark navy mane and tail, gold horn, gold hooves, a football at its feet.
> Keep the LEFT 40% of the image empty pitch (text goes there); put the scene in the right half. Size 1200 × 300.
