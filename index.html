A free daily football trivia game

Unicorner is a free online football trivia game for fans who know more than the obvious answers. Every day, you get five football questions and 30 seconds to name one valid player, club, country, manager or stadium. Popular answers score fewer points, while rare answers can earn a Screamer. Play the new daily football quiz, test your ball knowledge and challenge your friends to beat your score. No download or sign-up required.

Is Unicorner free?

Yes. The daily football quiz is completely free to play.

When is there a new game?

Five new football trivia questions are released every day.

How does scoring work?

Correct but obvious answers score fewer points. The rarest valid answers score the most.

Is Unicorner available in Spanish?

Yes. Play in Spanish at unicornergame.lol/es/, or tap the ES button at the top. The questions, ranking and leagues are the same in both languages, and you can switch back any time.

Is there a football Wordle?

Yes. Unicorner is a daily football game in the spirit of Wordle: one new game a day, the same five questions for everyone, and a score you can share. Instead of guessing a word, you name players, clubs or countries, and the rarer your answer, the more it scores.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Create a private league, send the link to your friends and compare your daily and weekly scores. Leagues need a free sign-in with your email; the daily game itself does not.

Is there a leaderboard?

Yes. There is a global ranking for each day's game and a weekly ranking that resets every Monday, and yesterday's winner is shown at the top. To appear on it, sign in and pick a nickname.

What is the highest score?

500. Each of the five questions is worth up to 100 points: 10 for the most obvious answers (a Tap-in), then 30, 60 and 85, and 100 for the rarest valid answers (a Screamer).

What is Infinity mode?

Infinity mode is endless practice with the same rules as the daily game: 30 seconds per question and rarer answers score more, but there is no ranking. You can try a short run now; the full endless version is coming soon.

What are kits?

Kits are outfits for your pixel unicorn. There are 40 to unlock by playing, through games played, streaks, Screamers and big scores. The rarest, the Cosmic GOAT, is only for players who win a day. If you are signed in, your kit shows on the leaderboard.
