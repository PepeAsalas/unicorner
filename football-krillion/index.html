How it plays

Five prompts a day

"Name a Brazilian who played for Real Madrid." "Name a club that won the Europa League." You get 30 seconds to name any answer that fits.

Rarer answers score more

Vinícius is a Tap-in worth 10. A player almost nobody remembers is a Screamer worth 100. Obvious answers count, but deep cuts win.

Same game for everyone

A new set every midnight, a daily ranking, streaks, and leagues with your mates. Free, no sign-up, in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

More than the daily game

Duels

1v1 against a friend, a player near your rating, or bots from Sunday League to Legends. Rarer answers hit harder.

Rosco and Millions

A 24-letter football alphabet against the clock, and a quiz where you spread a million over the answers and keep what lands on the right one.

Stable Hand

Football blackjack: every answer is a card worth its rarity. Get close to the target without going over.

Unicorner vs Krillion

Both reward knowing more than the obvious answer. Unicorner is only about football and soccer, gives you a fresh set of five prompts every day, and adds duels, streaks and leagues. Unicorner is an independent game and is not affiliated with Krillion.
