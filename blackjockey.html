How to play

Horacio deals one football category at a time. Name something that fits: it becomes a card, face down. Each card is worth a number (a stadium's capacity, the year a club was founded, a player's goals…), but you only see the numbers at the end.

After each card, take another or stand. Get as close to the target as you can. Go over and you bust: 0. Hit it exactly and it's a Black Jockey. Up to 6 cards, 45 seconds each. Same table for everyone today.

Prototype · the numbers come from public records and will be fact-checked before launch.
