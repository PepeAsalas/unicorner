How to play

Every answer is a card worth its rarity: the more obvious the answer, the less it's worth. Tap-in 10 · Header 30 · Volley 60 · Bicycle kick 85 · Screamer 100.

Get as close to the target as you can in up to 5 cards. Over the target, you bust. A wrong answer or running out of time is a foul: 0 points, and the card is gone. You can pass one question.

Horacio plays a hand too: one card up, the rest face down. Stand when you think you've got him, then he turns his cards. Hit the target exactly and it's a Full House.

Prototype · questions and rarities come from the Duels question bank.
