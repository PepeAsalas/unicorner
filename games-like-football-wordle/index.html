What makes Unicorner different

Most daily football games have one right answer. Unicorner has dozens, and the rarer your answer, the more it scores. "Name a Brazilian who played for Real Madrid": Vinícius scores a Tap-in, a player almost nobody remembers scores a Screamer. It rewards real ball knowledge, not just the obvious names.

Other kinds of daily football games

  • Guess-the-player games: work out today's mystery footballer from clues like club, league, nationality and position.
  • Football grid games: fill a grid with players who fit two clubs or categories at once.
  • "Name every…" quizzes: list every player in a famous squad or every winner of a trophy against the clock.
  • Higher or lower: guess which player has more goals, caps or a bigger transfer fee.

Unicorner sits between them: like a grid game you need players who fit the prompt, like a list quiz there are many correct answers, and like Wordle it's one game a day that you can share with friends.
