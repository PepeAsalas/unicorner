# The presenter: art prompts for ChatGPT

The presenter is a pixel-art unicorn who hosts Unicorner's quiz shows (the daily Rosco, and later Atrapa un millón).
He is a classic, confident game-show host (Steve Harvey energy) in a sharp suit, like our Boardroom kit.
He talks to the player like an NPC in an RPG: a portrait next to a text box, with a few frames per mood.

How to use these prompts:

1. Send **Prompt 1** first and keep regenerating until you love the character. Everything else copies it.
2. In the **same chat**, send **Prompt 2** once per row of the frame list below (or 2–3 rows at a time).
   Always say "same character as the reference sheet, same size and position".
3. Download each frame as a separate PNG with a transparent background, named exactly as in the list
   (for example `talk-1.png`), and send me the zip. I'll cut, align and wire them into the game.

Palette (our site colours, use these and nothing brighter):
paper `#fbf6e8` · cream `#f6eed8` · sand `#e9dfc2` · ink `#1d1a2b` · gold `#f2c230` · grass `#2f9a62` · clay red `#c8473b` · marine navy `#1d4486`

## Prompt 1: the character sheet

> Pixel art character design for a retro football quiz show. The host is a confident, classic TV game-show presenter
> who happens to be a unicorn: think of a big, charismatic Saturday-night quiz host with a booming laugh,
> like Steve Harvey hosting Family Feud. Shown from the chest up (a portrait bust, like an RPG dialogue portrait),
> facing slightly to the right.
> Style: chunky 16-bit pixel art, a 64×64 pixel grid scaled up crisply with no blur and no anti-aliasing,
> a 1-pixel dark ink outline (#1d1a2b) around everything, flat colours with one shade per colour, no gradients.
> Character: a broad, strong horse head with a square jaw, light grey-white coat with sand shading (#e9dfc2),
> a short, neatly cropped dark charcoal mane (no long flowing hair, no pastel colours), a thick, well-groomed
> dark moustache, heavy eyebrows, and a big confident grin. A short, solid gold horn (#f2c230), plain, no stripes.
> Outfit: a sharp, tailored marine-navy suit (#1d4486) with wide lapels, a crisp white shirt, a gold tie (#f2c230),
> a white pocket square and a gold football lapel pin. He holds a classic handheld microphone.
> No sparkles, no hearts, no rainbow, no glitter, no pink or purple. Serious suit, big personality.
> Plain transparent background. Make a character sheet with the neutral pose large in the centre, plus small views of
> the same character looking left and three-quarter, so I can keep him consistent.

## Prompt 2: one frame at a time

> Using the reference character above, draw the SAME unicorn presenter in exactly the same pixel art style,
> same 64×64 grid, same size, same position in the frame, same outfit and colours, transparent background.
> Only the face, hooves and microphone change. This frame: **[paste the description from the list]**.
