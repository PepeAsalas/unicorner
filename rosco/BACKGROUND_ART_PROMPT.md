# Rosco background: art prompt for ChatGPT

The page sits behind the letter wheel, Horacio Herradura's speech box and the answer box, so it must stay calm in the
middle and carry the detail at the edges. We use it in two versions, like the other backgrounds: tall (phones) and
wide (computers). Send me both PNGs and I'll make the .webp files and wire them in (`bg/rosco-tall.webp`, `bg/rosco-wide.webp`).

## Prompt (tall, for phones)

> Pixel art background for a retro TV football quiz show called "Rosco". A Saturday-night game-show studio seen from the
> audience: a dark studio with a glossy stage floor, and in the centre of the stage a big circular podium ring made of
> 25 square letter tiles (A to Z), unlit and slightly out of focus, as if waiting for the game to start.
> Behind the stage, a wall of chunky retro TV screens and stage lights, a small scoreboard with a clock, and football
> touches: a strip of pitch-green floor at the very front, a goal net hanging as stage decor, a gold trophy on a plinth
> at one side, a camera on a crane at the other. A few spotlights shine down in soft beams.
> Style: 16-bit pixel art, crisp pixels, no blur, no anti-aliasing, no text anywhere, flat colours with simple shading.
> Colours: mostly marine navy (#1d4486) and deep ink (#1d1a2b) for the studio, gold (#f2c230) for lights and the trophy,
> warm cream (#f6eed8) highlights, a touch of grass green (#2f9a62) and clay red (#c8473b). No pink, no purple, no neon.
> Keep the centre of the image dark and simple (the game's letter wheel and text boxes will sit on top of it);
> put the detail at the top and the edges. Portrait format, 1080 × 1920.

## Prompt (wide, for computers)

> Same scene, same style and colours, in landscape format 1920 × 1080: the same studio, wider, with the letter-tile ring
> in the centre, the TV-screen wall and stage lights across the top, the trophy on the left and the camera crane on the
> right. Keep a calm, dark area in the middle third where the game sits.

## Optional extra

> The same studio, same framing, but with the letter-tile ring lit up in gold and spotlights pointing at it, confetti
> in the air (gold and cream only). Used for the end screen when a player gets a great score.
