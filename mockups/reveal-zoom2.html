The beats

  • Crowd (0.4 s): a wide shot of 100 unicorns in real kits, idling and murmuring. Yours sits in the middle in your kit.
  • Wind-up (0.16 s): the camera eases back a touch, so the push has somewhere to come from.
  • Whoosh (0.6 to 1.2 s): one fast push-in that speeds up and then brakes. Speed lines and a little blur only during the fastest part. Everyone who said something else jumps out of shot, from the edges inwards.
  • Landing: the camera overshoots slightly and settles, and your unicorn squashes on impact. Bicycle and Screamer get a short freeze-frame before the stamp.
  • Screamer only: black bars slide in like a film, golden rays turn behind you, then a flash, confetti and a roar.
  • Your unicorn always has a gold outline and a light pool under it, so you can spot yourself in the crowd before the zoom.
  • Unicorns about 2× bigger than v1: around 40 px in the crowd and 170 px close up.
