How it plays

  • 0.0 s The crowd: wide shot of 100 horses on the pitch, your unicorn somewhere in the middle.
  • 0.5 s Whoosh. The camera rushes in on you, with a little motion blur during the fastest part. Horses peel away from the edges inwards, and the % drops with the zoom.
  • ~1.4 s Impact. The camera lands, the tier stamps in and the points count up.
  • Tier sets the depth: Tap-in 41% barely moves, Header 17%, Volley 6% ends with your little group, Bicycle 2% is nearly alone, Screamer 0.4% is the full push-in on you alone, with a flash, a gold stamp and a crowd roar.
  • About 2 seconds per question, and a tap skips straight to the result.
