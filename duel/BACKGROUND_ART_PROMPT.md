# Duels background: art prompts for ChatGPT

The Duels page puts a cream card (the duel, the battle, the results) in the middle of the screen. On computers there are
also two side panels (the season table on the left, your duels on the right). So every background must stay calm and
dark in the middle and carry its detail at the top, the bottom and the far edges.

Paste the **style block** first, then one **scene**. Ask for the tall version (phones) first; if you like it, ask for
the wide one. Send me the PNGs you pick and I'll turn them into `bg/duel-tall.webp` and `bg/duel-wide.webp` and wire them in.

## Style block (paste before every scene)

> Pixel art background for a football trivia game where two unicorn players face off 1 v 1 in a "duel".
> Style: 16-bit pixel art, crisp square pixels, no blur, no anti-aliasing, flat colours with simple shading,
> no text, no letters, no logos, no people's faces, no unicorns (the game draws its own characters on top).
> Colours: deep ink (#1d1a2b) and marine navy (#1d4486) for the dark areas, gold (#f2c230) for lights,
> clay red (#c8473b) for one side and cream (#fbf6e8) for highlights, a little grass green (#2f9a62) only where there is pitch.
> No pink, no purple, no neon. The scene is split into two sides, left and right, facing each other: a "versus" feeling.
> Keep the centre of the image dark and simple, because a cream card with the game sits on top of it.
> Put the detail at the top, the bottom and the far left and right edges.

## Scenes (pick one per request)

**A · Title fight.** A boxing-style arena at night where the ring is a small square of football pitch with corner flags
as ring posts and ropes between them. Seen from ringside, low. Stacked crowd in silhouette, camera flashes as tiny gold
pixels, two spotlights crossing in the middle, a big scoreboard hanging from the roof with two blank panels.
Left side lit warm gold, right side lit clay red.

**B · Penalty shootout.** A night stadium seen from the penalty spot, with two goals: one far left, one far right,
both glowing under floodlights, the halfway line running up the middle. Floodlight towers in the top corners with
chunky light beams. Crowd in the stands as rows of small coloured dots, half gold scarves, half red scarves.

**C · Arcade versus stage.** A retro 1990s fighting-game stage set in a football street: a cobbled square at dusk with
a painted wall of club scarves, a chain-link five-a-side cage behind, a moped and a stack of crates at the edges,
string lights across the top, a puddle reflecting the lights at the bottom. Two empty spots on the ground, left and
right, where the fighters would stand.

**D · The tunnel.** The players' tunnel before a derby, seen from inside looking out to the pitch: two lines of
small lights along the walls, one wall painted gold and one clay red, the bright pitch and floodlights framed at the
far end of the tunnel (small, high up in the image), club crests as blank shields on the walls.

**E · Rooftop cage at dusk.** A five-a-side pitch on a city rooftop at sunset, chain-link fence all around, city
skyline and water towers in the background, a big sunset sky in gold and red at the top, two small goals at the left
and right edges, floodlights just switching on.

## Sizes
